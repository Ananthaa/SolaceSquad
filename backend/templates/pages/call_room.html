Audio & Echo Help

Follow these simple steps to immediately resolve microphone feedback, echo, or background noise.

1
Wear Headphones / Earphones This is the single most effective way to eliminate echo. It physically isolates speaker output from your microphone.
2
Lower Speaker Volume If you are not using a headset, lower your device speaker volume to 40% - 50%. High volumes overwhelm echo-cancellation software.
3
Close Duplicate Tabs Check if you have the call room open in other browser tabs or windows, as this duplicates audio loops.
4
Refresh / Reload Page If audio becomes unstable or echo starts mid-call, refreshing the page re-initializes WebRTC audio cancellation.
