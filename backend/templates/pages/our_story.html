About Us

OUR STORY

"Nothing can dim the light that shines from within"
— Maya Angelou

We at SolaceSquad® believe that a healthy mind is the greatest treasure. We promise to be your best partner during your tough times. Take a step today with us and give us an opportunity to help you lead a better tomorrow.

Mental illnesses affect 19% of the adult population, 46% of teenagers and 13% of children each year. People struggling with their mental health may be in your family, live next door, teach your children, work in the next cubicle or sit in the same pew. However, only half of those affected receive treatment, often because of the stigma attached to mental health.

Mental wellbeing is crucial for overall health and quality of life. It encompasses emotional, psychological, and social aspects of well-being. Prioritizing mental health reduces stress, improves resilience, enhances relationships, and boosts productivity.

Personal well-being is essential for a fulfilling life. It encompasses physical, emotional, social, and mental health. Prioritizing personal well-being fosters resilience, happiness, and a balanced life.

We at SolaceSquad® try to address these concerns to the best of our ability, partnering with you and the best experts so that YOU have a BETTER Life.
