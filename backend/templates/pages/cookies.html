1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by your web browser when you visit a website. They contain a small amount of data, which often includes an anonymous unique identifier. They are sent back to the originating website on each subsequent visit and allow the website to recognise your browser.

Cookies serve many functions — they help websites remember who you are, keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how you interact with pages.

Session Cookies Temporary cookies that exist only while your browser is open. They are automatically deleted when you close your browser tab or window.
Persistent Cookies These remain on your device for a set period defined in the cookie. They activate each time you visit the website that created them and are used to remember your preferences across sessions.
First-Party Cookies Set directly by SolaceSquad® when you use our Platform.
Third-Party Cookies Set by our trusted service partners (e.g., Google Analytics, payment processors) when their features are active on our Platform.

4. HOW AND WHY WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies for the following legitimate purposes:

Authentication & Security: To keep you securely logged in during your session, prevent session hijacking, and protect form submissions from cross-site attacks.

Booking & Appointments: To maintain your booking state during the appointment scheduling flow, ensuring your selected time slot is preserved until the transaction completes.

Video Consultations: To manage real-time call session state and ensure continuity in video consultation rooms.

User Preferences: To remember your display preferences (e.g., dashboard settings, notification preferences) so you do not have to re-enter them each visit.

Platform Analytics & Improvement: To understand which features are used most, identify error-prone areas, and direct our development roadmap to best serve our users.

Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent sign-ups, spam form submissions, and automated bot activity through reCAPTCHA integration.

5. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES AND SERVICES

Certain features of the SolaceSquad® Platform are powered by trusted third-party providers who may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for details.

Google LLC (Analytics, Sign-In, reCAPTCHA)
Google sets cookies to analyse site usage (Google Analytics), allow Sign-In with Google (OAuth 2.0), and distinguish humans from bots (reCAPTCHA). These cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Payment Processors
When you complete a payment, our payment partner may set cookies to process the transaction securely and prevent fraud. These are essential for the payment flow and governed by the payment provider's privacy policy.

Communication & Email (SendGrid / Google Workspace)
Transactional emails sent by SolaceSquad® may include tracking pixels (standard for transactional email providers) that inform us whether an email was opened. You can disable email tracking by setting your email client to not load remote images.

Video Conferencing (WebRTC / Daily.co)
Our video consultation rooms use WebRTC and may store temporary data to maintain call state. This data is not used beyond the active session.

7. HOW TO MANAGE, OPT OUT, OR DELETE COOKIES

You have the right to accept, decline, or delete cookies at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent large portions of the Platform from functioning, including login and appointment booking.

Through your browser settings:

• Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions

• Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data

• Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All

• Microsoft Edge: Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data

• Opera: Settings → Advanced → Privacy & security → Site Settings → Cookies

For mobile devices:

• iOS (Safari): Settings → Safari → Block All Cookies

• Android (Chrome): Chrome → Settings → Site Settings → Cookies

Opt out of specific third-party tracking:

• Google Analytics: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

• Google Ad settings: adssettings.google.com

• Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org

9. DO NOT TRACK (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want your online activity tracked. There is currently no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals.

At present, SolaceSquad® does not alter its data collection and use practices in response to DNT signals. We will revisit this policy as industry standards evolve. You may also use the opt-out mechanisms described in Section 7 above to limit tracking.

10. CHILDREN AND COOKIES

The SolaceSquad® Platform is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly place cookies on the devices of minors. If you believe that a child under 18 has used the Platform in a manner that involved the collection of cookie data, please contact us immediately at info@solacesquad.com and we will take appropriate remedial action.

11. DATA RETENTION

Cookie data is retained for as long as the cookie is active (see Section 3 for specific durations). When a cookie expires or is deleted, associated data is no longer accessible to us. Analytics data aggregated from cookies may be retained for up to 26 months in accordance with Google Analytics' data retention settings.

SolaceSquad® does not retain raw cookie values in its own databases. The session token stored in your browser cookie is a reference to a server-side session, which is itself deleted upon logout or expiry.
