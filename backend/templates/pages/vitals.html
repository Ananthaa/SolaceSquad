Quick Setup — 4 Steps

Follow these steps before pressing Start Scan

1

☀️ Find Good Light

Sit facing a window or lamp so your face is evenly lit. Avoid light coming from behind you.

→ Watch for the ☀️ Good Light badge on the camera

2

📱 Hold Camera at Eye Level

Prop your phone so the camera is at eye level, roughly arm's length away (40–70 cm).

→ Don't angle the phone up from below — it blocks face detection

3

🎯 Centre Face in the Green Box

Look directly at the camera until the green box fits snugly around your face. Remove glasses if you can.

→ The box turns green when your position is good → then press Start

4

⏱️ Stay Still for 45 Seconds

Once scanning starts, don't move or speak. Breathe slowly and naturally. Relax your jaw and shoulders.

→ Even small head movements reduce accuracy

Wellness & Biometric Reference Guide

Physiological baselines, autonomic metrics, standard offsets & wellness insights

⚠️
General Wellness Reference: This reference guide provides standard physiological wellness ranges for informational and educational awareness. SolaceSquad is not a diagnostic device. Always consult a healthcare professional for clinical advice or treatment.
Vagal Tone Indicator

Heart Rate Variability (HRV - RMSSD)

Heart Rate Variability measures the precise variation in time (in milliseconds) between consecutive heartbeats. Solace Squad captures RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences), the clinical gold standard reflecting parasympathetic (vagus nerve) activity and cardiovascular recovery capacity.

How to Read Your HRV
Higher is generally better. Unlike resting heart rate, a higher HRV indicates that your nervous system is responsive, adaptable, and recovering efficiently. A low HRV indicates autonomic fatigue, systemic inflammation, chronic stress, or inadequate sleep.
Wellness Reference Spectrum
Depleted
< 20 ms
Severe fatigue, illness, high inflammation, or sleep deficit.
Fatigued
20 – 34 ms
Elevated sympathetic tone; recovery and restorative rest needed.
Balanced
35 – 59 ms
Healthy autonomic regulation and good daily readiness.
Optimal
60+ ms
Exceptional vagal recovery and peak physiological adaptability.
Standard Offset & Day-to-Day Interpretation
  • Population Median: Healthy adult resting RMSSD typically ranges between 35 and 65 ms. It naturally decreases by ~3–5 ms per decade of chronological age.
  • Personal Baseline Offset: Because genetic and biological variance between individuals is wide, your daily offset from your 7-day average is far more informative than an isolated snapshot.
  • Within ±10 ms: Reflects steady homeostasis.
  • Drop > 15 ms below baseline: Indicates incomplete recovery, impending viral infection, or acute sympathetic overdrive. Prioritize sleep and active recovery.
⚡ Recommended Interventions to Boost HRV:
• Coherent Breathing: 5.5 breaths/minute (4-second inhale, 6-second exhale) for 5 minutes elevates parasympathetic tone immediately.
• Sleep Consistency: Maintain regular bed and wake hours within a 30-minute window to stabilize autonomic circadian cycles.
• Hydration & Recovery: Ingest adequate water and electrolytes; avoid alcohol and heavy meals within 3 hours of sleep.
Autonomic Strain

Autonomic Stress Index (0 – 100)

The Autonomic Stress Index measures the ratio of sympathetic (fight-or-flight) arousal against parasympathetic brake activity. It is computed from instantaneous pulse rhythm regularity, micro-accelerations, and peripheral vasomotor responses.

How to Read Your Stress Index
Lower is calmer. A lower index signifies relaxed cardiovascular tone and dominant parasympathetic activity. Elevated scores reflect active mental focus, emotional load, physical strain, or stimulant intake.
Wellness Reference Spectrum
Calm & Restored
0 – 25
Deep parasympathetic rest; minimal cardiovascular tension.
Balanced
26 – 50
Healthy alert state for daytime focus and productivity.
Elevated Strain
51 – 75
Active sympathetic drive; take a 5-minute cognitive rest.
High Strain
76 – 100
Acute sympathetic overdrive or physical exhaustion.
Standard Offset & Resting Baseline
  • Resting Baseline: Under seated, quiet conditions, a healthy adult baseline is 20 – 35.
  • Diurnal Fluctuation: Midday jumps of +15 to +25 points are natural during complex meetings or work sprints.
  • Persistent Elevation Warning: If your resting score remains > 65 across morning or evening scans when quiet, your autonomic nervous system is stuck in sympathetic overdrive.
⚡ Recommended Interventions to Lower Stress:
• Box Breathing: Inhale 4s, Hold 4s, Exhale 4s, Hold 4s for 4 cycles to reset vagal inhibition.
• Screen Reset: Look at an object 20+ feet away for 20 seconds; stretch neck and shoulders.
• Caffeine Curfew: Avoid stimulants after 2:00 PM to protect parasympathetic evening recovery.
Arterial Compliance

Vascular Age (Years)

Vascular Age evaluates the physiological stiffness and compliance of your arterial tree. It is derived from rPPG pulse wave contour morphology, pulse wave transit characteristics, and pulse pressure ($PP = \text{Systolic} - \text{Diastolic}$).

How to Read Your Vascular Age
Compare against your chronological age. A vascular age equal to or younger than your biological age confirms supple, elastic arteries that efficiently buffer cardiac ejection. A higher vascular age indicates increased arterial stiffness or elevated pulse pressure.
Offset from Chronological Age
Youthful Arteries
≤ Age − 3 yrs
Excellent arterial compliance, pliable vascular wall elasticity.
On Target
Age ± 2 yrs
Arterial stiffness aligns with healthy normative benchmarks.
Elevated Stiffness
≥ Age + 3 yrs
Stiffened arterial wall; assess blood pressure and lifestyle factors.
Standard Offset & Acute Influences
  • Target Offset: Ideal offset is 0 to −5 years relative to chronological age.
  • Acute Fluctuations (+2 to +4 yrs): Mild dehydration, recent high-sodium meals, caffeine, or cold room temperatures cause transient vasoconstriction that temporarily raises estimated vascular age.
  • Long-Term Trends: Consistent positive offsets (+5y or higher) over months warrant regular cuff blood pressure checks and cardiovascular conditioning.
⚡ Recommended Interventions for Vascular Elasticity:
• Zone 2 Aerobic Exercise: 150 minutes weekly of low-intensity cardio stimulates endothelial nitric oxide production.
• Dietary Nitrates: Beets, arugula, spinach, and pomegranate support vascular vasodilation.
• Mineral Balance: Ensure adequate dietary potassium and magnesium to offset sodium intake.

Blood Pressure measures the lateral force exerted by circulating blood against the walls of major arteries during contraction (Systolic) and resting relaxation (Diastolic).

💡 Pulse Pressure (PP = Systolic − Diastolic)
A normal resting Pulse Pressure is 30 – 50 mmHg. Widening pulse pressures (> 55 mmHg) are a key clinical marker of large-artery stiffness and accelerated vascular aging.
