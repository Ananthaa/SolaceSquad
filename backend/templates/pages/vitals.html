Quick Setup — 4 Steps

Follow these steps before pressing Start Scan

1

☀️ Find Good Light

Sit facing a window or lamp so your face is evenly lit. Avoid light coming from behind you.

→ Watch for the ☀️ Good Light badge on the camera

2

📱 Hold Camera at Eye Level

Prop your phone so the camera is at eye level, roughly arm's length away (40–70 cm).

→ Don't angle the phone up from below — it blocks face detection

3

🎯 Centre Face in the Green Box

Look directly at the camera until the green box fits snugly around your face. Remove glasses if you can.

→ The box turns green when your position is good → then press Start

4

⏱️ Stay Still for 45 Seconds

Once scanning starts, don't move or speak. Breathe slowly and naturally. Relax your jaw and shoulders.

→ Even small head movements reduce accuracy
