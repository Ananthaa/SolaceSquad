Import Apple Health

Upload your iPhone's Apple Health export to import all your workouts and steps into SolaceSquad.

How to export from your iPhone
  1. 1 Open the Health app on your iPhone
  2. 2 Tap your profile picture (top right) → scroll down → "Export All Health Data"
  3. 3 Wait for the export to complete, then tap Share and save/send the apple_health_export.zip file to this device
  4. 4 Upload the .zip file below — or if you have extracted it, the Export.xml file
